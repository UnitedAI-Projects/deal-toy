Open Mantel

Model, inspect and organize

Create a project, develop parametric 3D models with your connected AI client, inspect previews and keep exports and manufacturing notes alongside each revision. Mantel uses build123d and OpenCascade for its CAD engine. AI tools connect through MCP with account-scoped API keys.

Sign in with Google

Mantel uses your Google account identifier, verified email address and name for sign-in and account access. It does not request Gmail or Drive access. Read our privacy policy for details about information handling and connected AI clients.

Early access

Mantel is an early-access service. Access to modeling and other capabilities is managed by an administrator. Engineering drawings and a complete manufacturing approval workflow are still planned. Have a qualified person review designs and manufacturing requirements before production.
